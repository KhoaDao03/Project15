METHOD
Same original buy quantity, purchase cost and fees for every candidate. First observed fresh valid held-side bid at/below threshold triggers an irrevocable hypothetical exit; first retained book after delay supplies bid depth, not the trigger price. Entire quantity must fit retained depth. Otherwise comparison is unavailable. Trades without a stop use recorded official settlement. No partial-fill or market-impact simulation.
Central delay 1 second; sensitivity 0.5 and 3 seconds. Actual hard-stop detection-to-acknowledgement timing on 295 recorded sells: median 0.54s, p90 2.48s, p95 3.23s. This excludes quote age and earlier unobserved crossings.
Common cohort requires all stops 40–70 cents and all tested delays to have adequate coverage from entry acknowledgement through hypothetical exit (or settlement if no exit), no valid-book gap over 5s, and next execution book within 2s of eligibility. Empty held-side books count as observed but cannot fill an exit. A stricter 2s coverage comparison is retained in results.json. Broader 5–80c scenarios were evaluated on this same cohort.
Recorded book samples may miss intervening crossings. Actual polling, queue delay, liquidity competition, fill confirmation, partial sales and order effects cannot be reconstructed exactly. Actual buy entry acknowledgement is used as exposure start; fills may precede it slightly.
Modeled sell fees use the repository FeeAccumulator at 0.07 and $0.0001 balance precision. Reference: https://kalshi.com/docs/kalshi-fee-schedule.pdf (general taker formula and no settlement fee). Actual buy fees are taken from exchange order records.
